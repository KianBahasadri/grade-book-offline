A LITTLE HELP GETTING STARTED

Download.

Not sure which to choose?
Start with PDF. It works on most phones, tablets, and computers.

How do I open my download?+

Find the file in your device’s Downloads folder and tap or double-click it.

PDF: open it with your browser or a PDF reader.
EPUB: open it in an EPUB reading app or transfer it to a compatible e-reader.
Word: open it with Word or a compatible document app.

How does the offline website work?+

Download the ZIP file, then choose “Extract” or “Unzip.” Open the grade-book folder and double-click index.html.

Keep the folder together: it contains the book and its pictures. You can read it in your browser without an internet connection.

What’s included in this edition?+

{{COVERAGE}}

Figures, tables, authorship, and references are included in PDF, EPUB, Word, and the offline website. The plain text file includes figure descriptions, but not the pictures.

Unfinished chapters and links to the older handbook are listed separately. External websites, videos, and interactive tools still need an internet connection.

Will my copy stay up to date?+

A downloaded copy stays as it was on its edition date. We check the original book every week and publish a new edition only when its content changes.

Return to this page to get the latest copy. You can also keep an earlier edition for reference.
