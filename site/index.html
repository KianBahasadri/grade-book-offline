About & help

Read it your way

PDF opens on most phones and computers. EPUB lets you adjust the text in a reading app. For the offline website, unzip the download and open grade-book/index.html. Keep its folder together.

{{COVERAGE}} Figures, tables, references, and original attribution are retained. Plain text includes image descriptions. Unfinished chapters and older-handbook links are listed separately; external websites and videos still need a connection.

We check weekly and publish only when the source changes. Return here for a newer copy.
