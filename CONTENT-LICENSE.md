The source requests this citation:

> Neumann I, Schünemann H, editors. The GRADE Book [Internet]. Version 1.0.
> Milan: GRADE Working Group; 2024 [updated 2024 Sep; cited date].
> Available from: https://book.gradepro.org/. ISBN 979-12-243-3917-5.

Use the edition's capture date as the access date. Individual chapter authors,
citations, modification dates, and graphics attribution are retained in each
edition, along with the source's About page.

These are unofficial, noncommercial format conversions. Changes are limited to
formatting, navigation, and packaging; the book's prose is not rewritten or
translated. No endorsement by the GRADE Working Group or Evidence Prime is
implied. Third-party notices remain applicable.

Only published English chapters in the current GRADE Book are included. Entries
that link to the older handbook and unfinished entries are listed separately.
External websites and videos remain online links. The TXT edition contains text
and figure descriptions, rather than the figures themselves.

The website’s bundled `official-cover.png` is the original cover asset linked by
the source’s About page, captured on 20 October 2026. New website builds take the
cover from the latest published offline edition. The image is retained unchanged;
the website presents it on a three-dimensional book. IBM Plex Sans is distributed
under its bundled OFL license, and Lucide icons under their bundled license.
